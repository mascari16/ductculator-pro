    Key guide

    Entry5 Feet 4 Inch 1 / 2 = 5′ 4-1/2″. Press Feet or Inch after an entry or answer to convert it, and press it again to see the decimal. A whole number with one unit pressed twice (14 Feet Feet) is square, three times cubic. No denominator uses your fraction setting.
    x y r θType a value then press to store. Press with nothing typed to solve. Enter any two. θ takes degrees, an inch rise (9 Inch θ = 9/12), or a grade (75 Conv % θ). Press θ again: % grade, slope, pitch. Press r again: plumb, level.
    OffsetStore x = length, y = offset, Conv 4 = end (a). Then Conv ( and 1 CR · 2 WL · 3 HEEL · 4 THRT · 5 THET.
    Fan lawsConv 4 a (CFM) · Conv 5 b · Conv 7 a new · Conv 8 b new. Enter three, then Conv x Fan Law 1 (RPM) · Conv y Fan Law 2 (SP) · Conv r Fan Law 3 (BHP).
    VP / FPMValue, Conv 0, then 1 FPM from VP · 2 VP from FPM · 3 m/s from kPa · 4 kPa from m/s.
    Circle / ArcDiameter Circ (or a radius then Conv θ). Repeat Circ: circumference, area. Then an angle or arc length and Conv Circ. Repeat it for chord, segment area, pie area, segment rise and arched-wall stud lengths (AW1, AW2… at the jack spacing).
    Seg Radiusx = chord, y = rise, then Conv θ gives radius. Repeat for angle, arc, segment area, pie area. A value then Conv θ stores a radius for Circ/Arc. Conv π = ArcK (π/180).
    Col / ConeDiameter Circ, height in y, then Conv ). Press ) to step: column volume, column side area, cone volume, cone side area.
    TriangleSides Conv 4 5 6 (a b c), then Conv 9 and 1–3 angles A B C · 4 area · 5–7 sides.
    StairsRise in y and/or run in x (or a rise then Stair), then keep pressing Stair. It steps through riser height, # risers, riser over/under, tread, # treads, tread over/under, stairwell opening, stringer, incline, run and rise, then the stored riser, tread, headroom and floor thickness. A value then Conv Stair stores the riser height you want.
    RoofPitch in θ (9 Inch θ = 9/12) and run in x. r gives the common rafter. Hip/V steps through hip length, plumb, level, check. Jack steps through the jacks, then the plumb, level and check cuts. A value then Jack sets the on-center spacing (default 16″). Irregular: 8 Inch Conv Hip/V stores an 8/12 irregular pitch. Hip/V then gives the irregular hip, and Conv Jack the jacks on the irregular side. On/C On/C clears it.
    MemoryM+ adds · Conv M+ subtracts · Rcl M+ recalls · Rcl On/C clears. Conv 1–3 stores M1–M3; Rcl 1–3 recalls. Conv Rcl swaps M+ with the display. Rcl then any register key shows it.
    SettingsConv = opens settings. +/− changes the value, = moves to the next setting, On/C exits. In order: 1 fraction resolution, 2 area display, 3 volume display, 4 tread width, 5 headroom, 6 floor thickness, 7 jack order, 8 irregular jack spacing, 9 exponent, 10 meter decimals, 11 degree decimals, 12 order of operations or chain, 13 standard or constant fractions.
