Using the wheel

  1. Turn the disc so the index at the top points at your airflow on the blue scale.
  2. Round size: find your friction rate on the disc's left edge; the size is on the red scale right next to it.
  3. Velocity: find the duct size on the disc's bottom edge; the velocity is on the green scale next to it.
  4. Drag the red hairline to read straight across any two scales.
